Deflect: privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Deflect is a browser game. You can play it on game sites such as Poki and CrazyGames, and at this address. To play online it connects to one game server, run by Deflect's developer and hosted by Fly.io. This page explains what that server keeps about you, and why. It is short because the game keeps very little.

What the game server keeps

  • The fighter name you choose. Other players see it, in the lobby and on the ranked ladder. Names are checked against a list of offensive words, which are starred out.
  • A random account key. The game makes it the first time you play and keeps it in your browser's local storage (on CrazyGames, in CrazyGames' own save), so the server knows it's you next time. It isn't linked to who you are.
  • Your progress: your rating, wins and losses, coins, the fighters and arenas you've unlocked, whether you've done the tutorial, and when you first and last played.

That's all. The game doesn't ask for your email address, real name, age or location, has no sign-up or login, takes no payments, sets no cookies, and runs no analytics or tracking.

While you play

Your moves in a match are sent to the server and on to the other players in it. Like any website, the server sees your IP address to send the game to you. It doesn't store it, but Fly.io, which runs the server, may keep it for a short time in its network logs (see Fly.io's privacy policy).

In the Poki version there is no chat between players. On other sites, lobby chat goes to the players in the same lobby, with offensive words starred out. Chat isn't stored.

Ads

Ads in the game are shown by the site you play on (Poki or CrazyGames), under that site's own privacy policy. The game server gets nothing from them except, when an ad pays coins, that it played through, so it can pay you.

Who sees it

Your fighter name, rank, rating and wins and losses are public to other players. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone else, apart from Fly.io as the server's host.

How long it's kept, and deleting it

Your progress is kept so it's there when you come back. Clearing your browser's storage for the game starts you afresh, and your old progress can no longer be reached. To have it deleted from the server, write to the address below with your fighter name.

Children

Deflect doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included. A parent who wants a child's progress removed can write to the address below.
